# NHS Imaging Waiting Lists: February 2025 vs February 2026

Excel analysis of NHS England open data comparing diagnostic imaging waiting lists across two years.

## Question
How did imaging waiting lists in England change between February 2025 and February 2026?

## Data
NHS England, *Monthly Diagnostics Waiting Times and Activity* (February 2025 and February 2026), national figures. Available at england.nhs.uk/statistics. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## Method
- Extracted national waiting list figures for MRI, CT, non-obstetric ultrasound, barium enema and DEXA for February 2025 and February 2026.
- Chart 1: imaging waiting lists by test, February 2025.
- Chart 2: imaging waiting lists by test, February 2026.
- Built a comparison table of the two months, calculating the change and percentage change for each test.
- Chart 3: February 2025 vs February 2026 waiting lists side by side for each test and summary.


## Findings
- Waiting lists grew for four of the five tests.
- MRI rose 16.0% (332,575 to 385,738), non-obstetric ultrasound 11.2% (580,477 to 645,515) and CT 6.1% (192,176 to 203,873).
- Barium enema rose 33.5%, but from a small base (3,218 to 4,295).
- DEXA was the only test to fall (59,433 to 58,118, -2.2%).
- Across the five tests combined, the waiting list grew by about 11%.
- Ultrasound had the largest waiting list in both years.

## Limitations
- Only two single months are compared, so seasonal effects may influence the results.
- The data shows waiting list size, not how long patients waited.
- Figures are as published by NHS England and may be revised.
